# 16. Training

Everything sits behind `<Space>p`, out of the way of normal editing.

1. Hardtime is on. In a code file, press `j` five times in a row, and from
   the fourth on it blocks the key and suggests something better (`5j`, `}`,
   `<CR>`). `<Space>pr` shows the report of bad habits and `<Space>pd` turns
   it on and off.
2. Precognition shows in the gutter and in the text where each motion leads.
   `<Space>ph` shows it once (peek) and `<Space>pH` keeps it on.
3. `<Space>pp` starts an nvim-training session, `<Space>pS` stops it and
   `<Space>pa` analyses your performance.
4. `<Space>pb` opens the VimBeBetter games and `<Space>pT` opens VimTeacher.
